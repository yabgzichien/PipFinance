Privacy Policy for Pip

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Pip ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal budgeting and expense-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it's stored, and who else (if anyone) sees it.

1. Data stored on your device

Pip is built to work primarily on-device. Your transactions, budgets, commitments, net worth entries, and category tags are stored locally in a SQLite database on your phone, using your device's secure storage where appropriate. You don't need to create an account or provide any API keys to use the app: Pip has no server that stores your financial data, and no copy of your data lives on our infrastructure.

2. Data you choose to share with third-party services

Some optional features send specific data to third-party providers in order to work. These only run when you use the corresponding feature (e.g. scanning a receipt or turning on cloud backup).

More on cloud backup. This feature is off until you turn it on and sign in to Google. Pip asks Google for the narrowest possible permission — access limited to a hidden folder that Pip itself creates. Pip cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Drive. The backup is stored under your own Google account and is governed by Google's terms and your own Drive settings; you can delete it at any time from within Pip or by revoking Pip's access in your Google account settings.

3. Crash diagnostics

When Pip crashes or hits an internal error, it sends an anonymous technical report so the problem can be found and fixed. This is the only data Pip transmits that you did not explicitly ask it to send, so here is exactly what it contains.

What is sent: the type of error (for example TypeError), the location in Pip's own program code where it occurred, the app version, your device model and operating system version, and a randomly generated installation identifier.

What is never sent: your transactions, amounts, balances, merchant names, category names, receipts, photos, notes, contacts, or any other content you have entered or captured. Where an internal error message could contain such details, Pip removes them on the device before the report is sent, and for errors raised inside features that handle your financial data Pip discards the error message entirely and reports only a fixed label naming the feature.

The installation identifier is a random value generated on your phone. It is not derived from your device's hardware, advertising ID, phone number, email, or any other identifier, and it is not linked to you personally. Its only purpose is to distinguish one phone crashing repeatedly from many phones each crashing once. If you turn crash diagnostics off, this identifier is deleted; if you later turn it back on, a new unrelated one is generated.

Turning it off: crash diagnostics are on by default and can be switched off at any time in Settings → Data → Crash Diagnostics. When off, nothing is transmitted at all.

Crash reports are processed on our behalf by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), whose servers are located in the United States. Reports are used solely to diagnose and fix faults in the app. They are not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any commercial purpose, and they are not sold or shared with anyone else.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't run advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • We don't use behavioural or usage analytics. Pip does not record which screens you visit, what you tap, how long you use the app, or how you navigate it.
  • We don't require you to create an account or provide your identity to use the app.

5. Device permissions

  • Camera / Photo library: to capture or import a receipt, statement, or e-Invoice image. Only used when you actively choose to scan or attach something.
  • Notifications: for reminders (e.g. logging a transaction, upcoming commitments). Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device.
  • Google account access (optional): requested only if you turn on cloud backup, and limited to Pip's own hidden folder in your Drive.

6. Your control over your data

Because your data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing entries in the app or uninstalling the app, which erases the local database. In addition:

  • If you have used cloud backup, delete the backup from within Pip, or revoke Pip's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • If you want no crash reports sent, switch off Settings → Data → Crash Diagnostics.
  • If you used a document-scanning feature, review section 2 for what may have already been sent to a third-party provider for that specific request.

7. Children's privacy

Pip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
